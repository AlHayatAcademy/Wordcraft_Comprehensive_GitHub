# First conversations|Start here|Say what you need and understand simple requests.|chat
hello|interjection|A friendly word used when you meet or greet someone.|سلام؛ ہیلو|say hello|Say hello to your new classmate.|Use hello as a greeting, not as a farewell.
name|noun|The word or words used to identify a person or thing.|نام|full name|Please write your full name on this form.|A full name usually includes a given name and a family name.
please|adverb|A word that makes a request more polite.|براہ کرم|please help|Please help me find the classroom.|Put please at the beginning or end of a polite request.
thank|verb|To tell someone that you are grateful for something.|شکریہ ادا کرنا|thank someone|I want to thank you for your help.|Say thank you, or thank someone for something.
help|verb|To make it easier for someone to do something.|مدد کرنا|help someone|Can you help me carry this bag?|Say help someone do something or help someone with something.
need|verb|To require something because it is necessary.|ضرورت ہونا|need help|I need help with this question.|Need describes something necessary; want describes a wish.
# People around you|Start here|Talk about people and everyday relationships.|people
friend|noun|A person you know well and like spending time with.|دوست|close friend|My close friend lives near our school.|A close friend is someone you know particularly well.
family|noun|A group of people related to one another.|خاندان|family member|Each family member helped prepare the meal.|A family can include relatives beyond parents and children.
neighbour|noun|A person who lives near you.|پڑوسی|next-door neighbour|Our next-door neighbour watered our plants.|Neighbour is British spelling; neighbor is American spelling.
teacher|noun|A person whose work is to help others learn.|استاد|English teacher|Our English teacher explained the new word.|Use teach for the action and teacher for the person.
student|noun|A person who is learning at a school, college or other place.|طالب علم|new student|The new student asked where the library was.|Student can refer to learners of different ages.
guest|noun|A person invited to visit or stay with someone.|مہمان|welcome a guest|We welcome every guest with a cup of tea.|A guest is invited; a customer pays for a service.
# Everyday actions|Start here|Use practical verbs in simple sentences.|spark

leave|verb|To go away from a place.|روانہ ہونا؛ چلے جانا|leave home|We leave home at eight every morning.|Say leave a place, without from when naming the place directly.
wait|verb|To stay until someone arrives or something happens.|انتظار کرنا|wait for someone|Please wait for me outside the school.|Use wait for a person or thing.
share|verb|To let another person use or have part of something.|بانٹنا؛ شریک کرنا|share something|I share my lunch with my friend.|Use share something with someone.
# Time and routine|Start here|Describe when things happen and how long they last.|clock
early|adverb|Before the usual or expected time.|جلدی؛ وقت سے پہلے|arrive early|We arrive early so we can find our seats.|Early means before the expected time; soon means after a short time.
late|adverb|After the usual or expected time.|دیر سے|arrive late|The bus arrived late because of the rain.|Late describes timing; lately means recently.
usually|adverb|On most occasions, though not every time.|عام طور پر|usually walk|I usually walk to school with my sister.|Usually often goes before the main verb.
often|adverb|On many occasions.|اکثر|often visit|We often visit our grandparents on Sunday.|Often indicates frequency, not how long a visit lasts.
before|preposition|Earlier than a particular time or event.|سے پہلے|before class|I review my notes before class.|Before can introduce a time, an event or a clause.
after|preposition|Later than a particular time or event.|کے بعد|after lunch|We practise English after lunch.|After answers when; afterwards means at a later time.
# Places and directions|Start here|Find your way and describe familiar places.|map

between|preposition|In the space separating two people or things.|کے درمیان|between two buildings|The shop is between the bank and the school.|Between can also describe separate relationships among more than two things.
opposite|preposition|Facing someone or something on the other side.|کے سامنے|opposite the station|The café is opposite the station.|Opposite describes location here, not a contrary opinion.
entrance|noun|A door or other place through which you enter.|داخلے کا راستہ|main entrance|Meet me at the main entrance of the library.|Entrance is where you go in; exit is where you go out.
corner|noun|A place where two sides or streets meet.|کونا؛ موڑ|street corner|Turn left at the next street corner.|A street corner is a meeting point of streets.
direction|noun|The way in which someone or something moves or points.|سمت|right direction|Are we walking in the right direction?|Directions in the plural can mean instructions for finding a place.
# Food and daily needs|Start here|Talk about food, drinks and simple preferences.|cup
meal|noun|Food eaten at a regular time, such as breakfast or dinner.|کھانا|evening meal|We eat our evening meal together.|A meal is an occasion of eating, not a single ingredient.
fresh|adjective|Recently made, grown or obtained and still in good condition.|تازہ|fresh bread|The bakery sells fresh bread every morning.|Fresh can describe food that is not old or stale.
hungry|adjective|Feeling that you need or want to eat.|بھوک لگنا|feel hungry|I feel hungry after the long walk.|Say I am hungry or I feel hungry.
thirsty|adjective|Feeling that you need or want to drink.|پیاس لگنا|feel thirsty|You may feel thirsty after playing outside.|Thirsty describes a need for a drink, not food.
order|verb|To ask for food or another item to be supplied.|منگوانا؛ آرڈر دینا|order a meal|We order a meal at the café.|In a café, order means request something you will usually pay for.
prefer|verb|To like or choose one thing more than another.|ترجیح دینا|prefer tea|I prefer tea to coffee.|Use prefer one thing to another, rather than than.
# Buying and paying|Everyday English|Ask about prices and make practical decisions.|bag
price|noun|The amount of money needed to buy something.|قیمت|check the price|Check the price before you buy the bag.|Price is what a buyer pays; cost can include production or other expenses.
change|noun|Money returned when you pay more than the amount due.|بقایا رقم|receive change|I paid with a large note and received change.|Change is usually uncountable when it means money returned.
receipt|noun|A document showing that you have paid for something.|رسید|keep the receipt|Keep the receipt in case you return the shirt.|A receipt records payment; a bill usually requests it.
afford|verb|To have enough money or time for something.|استطاعت ہونا|afford a ticket|I cannot afford a ticket for that concert.|Afford is often used with can or cannot.
discount|noun|A reduction in the usual price.|قیمت میں کمی؛ رعایت|offer a discount|The shop offers a discount to students.|A discount reduces a price; it does not mean an item is free.
return|verb|To take something back to the place where you obtained it.|واپس کرنا|return an item|You can return an item if it is damaged.|Return an item describes giving it back, not receiving a refund automatically.
# Travel plans|Everyday English|Plan a journey and respond to changes.|map
journey|noun|An act of travelling from one place to another.|سفر|long journey|Take some water for the long journey.|Journey focuses on travel from one place to another.
destination|noun|The place you are travelling to.|منزل|reach a destination|We will reach our destination before sunset.|A destination is an end point, not the route used to reach it.
departure|noun|The act or scheduled time of leaving a place.|روانگی|departure time|Check the departure time on your ticket.|Departure contrasts with arrival.
delay|noun|A period of waiting that makes something happen later than planned.|تاخیر|unexpected delay|An unexpected delay made us miss the first lesson.|Delay may be a noun or a verb.
reserve|verb|To arrange for something to be kept for your later use.|پہلے سے محفوظ کرانا|reserve a seat|You can reserve a seat online.|Reserve means arrange in advance; pay if the booking rules require it.
route|noun|The way taken to reach a place.|راستہ|shorter route|This map shows a shorter route to the station.|Route is the way taken; destination is where you are going.
# Feelings and support|Everyday English|Describe feelings and respond thoughtfully.|heart
calm|adjective|Not worried, angry or excited.|پرسکون|stay calm|Try to stay calm before the interview.|Calm describes a state; calmly describes how you act.
worried|adjective|Feeling uneasy because something bad might happen.|فکرمند|worried about|I am worried about missing the bus.|Use worried about a situation, or worried that something may happen.
proud|adjective|Feeling pleased about an achievement or someone you value.|فخر محسوس کرنا|proud of|She felt proud of her careful work.|Use proud of someone or something.
disappointed|adjective|Unhappy because something was not as good as expected.|مایوس|disappointed with|He was disappointed with the result but tried again.|Disappointed concerns an unmet expectation, not every kind of sadness.
encourage|verb|To give someone confidence or support to do something.|حوصلہ افزائی کرنا|encourage someone|We encourage each other to practise English.|Use encourage someone to do something.
patient|adjective|Able to wait or deal with difficulty without becoming angry.|صابر؛ تحمل کرنے والا|patient with|Our teacher is patient with new learners.|Patient is an adjective here; a patient can also mean a person receiving healthcare.
# Solving small problems|Everyday English|Explain a problem and take useful action.|tool
notice|verb|To become aware of something by seeing or hearing it.|دھیان جانا؛ محسوس کرنا|notice a mistake|Did you notice a mistake in my answer?|Notice means become aware; notify means tell someone formally.
repair|verb|To make something damaged work properly again.|مرمت کرنا|repair a bicycle|The mechanic can repair my bicycle today.|Repair is more specific than change or replace.
replace|verb|To put a different thing in the place of another.|بدلنا|replace a battery|We need to replace the battery in the clock.|Replace means substitute something, rather than mend the original.
solution|noun|A way of solving a problem.|حل|find a solution|We worked together to find a solution.|Use a solution to a problem.
careful|adjective|Giving attention to avoid mistakes or harm.|محتاط|be careful|Be careful when you cross this busy road.|Careful describes the person; carefully describes an action.
urgent|adjective|Needing attention or action very soon.|فوری توجہ کا محتاج|urgent request|This urgent request needs an answer today.|Urgent concerns time and priority, not necessarily severity.
# Making plans|Everyday English|Set priorities and work towards a realistic goal.|clock
goal|noun|Something you want to achieve.|مقصد؛ ہدف|set a goal|Set a goal you can reach this week.|A goal is a desired result; a plan describes how to reach it.
prepare|verb|To get ready for an activity or event.|تیاری کرنا|prepare for|I prepare for each lesson by reading my notes.|Prepare for an event, or prepare something you will use.
schedule|noun|A plan that shows when activities will happen.|اوقات کا منصوبہ|study schedule|My study schedule includes a short break.|A schedule organises time; a list need not include times.
priority|noun|Something more important than other things and dealt with first.|ترجیح|top priority|Finishing the assignment is my top priority today.|Priorities can change as needs change.
organise|verb|To arrange things or activities in a useful order.|منظم کرنا|organise notes|I organise my notes by topic.|Organise is British spelling; organize is also standard.
complete|verb|To finish doing or making something.|مکمل کرنا|complete a task|Please complete the task before the lesson ends.|Complete is a verb here; it can also mean whole as an adjective.
# Clear conversations|Everyday English|Explain your point and understand another speaker.|chat
explain|verb|To make something clear by giving information about it.|وضاحت کرنا|explain an idea|Can you explain this idea in simpler words?|Explain something to someone, not explain someone something.
describe|verb|To say what a person, place or thing is like.|بیان کرنا|describe a place|Describe a place you enjoy visiting.|Describe gives details; explain often gives reasons or how something works.
agree|verb|To have the same opinion as someone.|متفق ہونا|agree with|I agree with your suggestion about the meeting.|Agree with a person or view; agree to a proposal or request.
suggest|verb|To offer an idea for someone to consider.|تجویز دینا|suggest a change|I suggest a change to our study plan.|Use suggest doing something, rather than suggest someone to do it.
clarify|verb|To make something easier to understand.|واضح کرنا|clarify a point|Please clarify the final point in your answer.|Clarify helps resolve uncertainty or ambiguity.
respond|verb|To give an answer or reaction.|جواب دینا؛ ردعمل دینا|respond to|Please respond to the invitation by Friday.|Use respond to a person, message or event.
# Learning effectively|Study and work|Understand common classroom and learning language.|book
focus|verb|To give most of your attention to something.|توجہ دینا|focus on|Try to focus on one task at a time.|Use focus on a task or topic.
review|verb|To look at something again to remember or assess it.|دہرانا؛ دوبارہ جائزہ لینا|review notes|I review my notes the day after each lesson.|Review can mean study again or assess the quality of something.
practice|noun|Repeated activity intended to improve a skill.|مشق|regular practice|Regular practice helps me remember new words.|In British English, practice is the noun and practise is the verb.
progress|noun|Movement towards improvement or a goal.|پیش رفت|make progress|You can make progress with a little study each day.|Progress is usually uncountable: make progress, not make a progress.

# Working together|Study and work|Take responsibility and collaborate on a task.|people

responsibility|noun|A duty to deal with something or take care of someone.|ذمہ داری|take responsibility|Each team member must take responsibility for one task.|Responsibility may mean a duty or accountability for an action.
contribute|verb|To give time, effort, money or ideas to something.|حصہ ڈالنا|contribute to|Everyone can contribute to the discussion.|Use contribute to an activity or result.
cooperate|verb|To work with others towards a shared purpose.|تعاون کرنا|cooperate with|We cooperate with another class on the project.|Use cooperate with someone or cooperate on a project.
deadline|noun|The latest time by which something must be done.|آخری مقررہ وقت|meet a deadline|We planned our work carefully to meet the deadline.|Meeting a deadline means finishing by that time.
reliable|adjective|Able to be trusted to do what is expected.|قابل اعتماد|reliable source|Use a reliable source when you check a fact.|Reliable describes dependable people, information or equipment.
# Reading for meaning|Study and work|Distinguish ideas, facts and supporting details.|book
context|noun|The surrounding words or situation that help explain meaning.|سیاق و سباق|understand the context|Read the whole sentence to understand the context.|Context helps you choose among different senses of a word.
detail|noun|A small piece of information about something.|تفصیل|important detail|Underline each important detail in the passage.|A detail supports a broader description or main idea.
purpose|noun|The reason for doing something or the intended result.|مقصد|main purpose|The main purpose of the notice is to explain a change.|Purpose answers why something is done.
opinion|noun|A belief or judgement that someone holds.|رائے|personal opinion|The final sentence expresses a personal opinion.|An opinion can be supported by evidence but is not automatically a fact.
evidence|noun|Information or observations that support or challenge a claim.|ثبوت؛ شواہد|supporting evidence|The writer gives supporting evidence for the claim.|Evidence is normally uncountable and may vary in strength.
infer|verb|To reach a conclusion from available information.|استنباط کرنا|infer a meaning|We can infer a meaning from the surrounding sentences.|A reader infers; a speaker or writer may imply.
# Comparing ideas|Study and work|Describe similarities, differences and significance.|compare
similar|adjective|Having features that are alike.|ملتا جلتا|similar to|This word is similar to one we learned yesterday.|Use similar to, rather than similar with.
contrast|verb|To compare things in order to show their differences.|فرق نمایاں کرنا|contrast two views|We contrast two views to show how they differ.|Contrast focuses on differences; compare can include similarities.
benefit|noun|An advantage or helpful result.|فائدہ|main benefit|The main benefit of the new timetable is a longer lunch break.|A benefit is a positive result, not simply any result.
limitation|noun|A restriction or weakness that reduces what something can do.|حد؛ کمی|important limitation|The small sample is an important limitation of the study.|A limitation does not necessarily make a study worthless.
significant|adjective|Important or large enough to deserve attention.|اہم؛ قابل توجہ|significant difference|There is a significant difference in the two accounts.|In statistics, significant has a technical meaning distinct from important.
alternative|noun|Another possible choice or way of doing something.|متبادل|practical alternative|Walking is a practical alternative to taking the bus.|An alternative is an option, not always the opposite.
# Writing with a clear structure|Study and work|Build a paragraph and connect your ideas.|pen
introduce|verb|To present a topic or idea for the first time.|متعارف کرانا|introduce a topic|Use your first paragraph to introduce the topic.|Introduce starts discussion; it does not need to explain every detail.
develop|verb|To make an idea fuller or more detailed.|تفصیل سے آگے بڑھانا|develop an idea|Use an example to develop the idea in your paragraph.|Develop can describe ideas, abilities or physical growth.
support|verb|To provide reasons or evidence for a statement.|تائید کرنا؛ دلیل سے تقویت دینا|support a claim|Use evidence to support your claim.|Support has other senses, including help or hold something up.
however|adverb|A word that introduces a contrast with what has just been said.|تاہم|however,|The plan is useful; however, it needs more time.|Use punctuation to separate however when it links contrasting statements.
therefore|adverb|For that reason or as a result.|اس لیے؛ لہٰذا|therefore,|The road was closed; therefore, we took another route.|Therefore introduces a consequence, not a contrast.
conclude|verb|To end a discussion or reach a judgement after considering information.|نتیجہ اخذ کرنا؛ اختتام کرنا|conclude a discussion|We conclude the discussion by summarising the main points.|Make clear whether conclude means finish or reach a judgement.
# Questions and research|Study and work|Understand how a simple investigation is organised.|search

method|noun|A planned way of doing something.|طریقہ|research method|The report explains the research method clearly.|A method states how work is carried out.
sample|noun|A smaller group or amount selected from a larger one for study.|نمونہ|small sample|The survey used a small sample of local residents.|A sample may not represent the whole population well.
observe|verb|To watch or examine something carefully.|مشاہدہ کرنا|observe a change|We observe a change in the plant each day.|Observation records what is noticed; interpretation explains it.

interpret|verb|To explain or decide the meaning of something.|معنی کی وضاحت کرنا؛ تعبیر کرنا|interpret a result|Researchers interpret a result using the available evidence.|Different interpretations need to be assessed against evidence.
# Evaluating an argument|Advanced reading|Assess claims without confusing confidence with evidence.|scale
claim|noun|A statement that someone says is true, which may need evidence.|دعویٰ|support a claim|The writer uses figures to support the claim.|A claim needs evaluation; it is not necessarily false.
assumption|noun|Something accepted as true without being established in that discussion.|مفروضہ|underlying assumption|The underlying assumption is that every student has internet access.|Identify an assumption before relying on it.
justify|verb|To give reasons showing why a view or action is reasonable.|جواز پیش کرنا|justify a decision|The school must justify its decision to change the timetable.|Justify means give reasons, not merely repeat a decision.
valid|adjective|Well founded or acceptable under the relevant rules or reasoning.|درست؛ معتبر|valid reason|Lack of transport is a valid reason for requesting a later start.|The technical meaning of valid depends on the field.

evaluate|verb|To judge the quality, value or effectiveness of something using criteria.|جانچنا؛ قدر و معیار کا جائزہ لینا|evaluate evidence|We evaluate the evidence before accepting the conclusion.|Evaluation includes a judgement; description alone does not.
# Precision and uncertainty|Advanced reading|Make careful statements about evidence and certainty.|scale

tentative|adjective|Not final or certain and open to change.|عارضی؛ غیر حتمی|tentative conclusion|This tentative conclusion needs further evidence.|Tentative signals caution rather than a settled judgement.
plausible|adjective|Seeming reasonable or possible on the available information.|قرین قیاس|plausible explanation|The writer offers a plausible explanation for the delay.|Plausible does not mean proven true.

# From evidence to insight|Advanced reading|Bring ideas together and recognise their limits.|bulb
