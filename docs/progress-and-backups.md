# Progress, scheduling and recovery

The `wordcraft-v1` storage key and backup `version: 1` remain unchanged. `schemaVersion: 2` adds `senses`, `selectedSenses`, selected teaching contexts, drafts and a revision number. Historical word records retain their IDs, scheduling stage, attempts, correct answers and bookmarks. Historical expression records retain saved status and scores. Notes retain their text; new notes can include a sense ID and revision link.

An old score does not identify which sense was practised. Migration therefore leaves `senses` empty for old backups; it never copies a word score into a guessed meaning. Exact new sense progress begins with an explicit attempt. Core/topic route aliases continue to resolve. Selected taxonomy senses are preserved as small context snapshots so an offline review does not require the full taxonomy. Unknown legacy word senses remain historical practice, not new sense evidence.

The shared scheduler is a transparent local heuristic, not an established proprietary or validated adaptive algorithm. Successful new/due attempts advance through 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60 and 120 days, at most once per UTC day. An error schedules a ten-minute retry. Correcting an error schedules a one-day check without increasing the stage. Assisted and productive activities do not advance intervals. Attempt counts may increase on repeat practice; interval stages do not inflate with repeated correct same-day answers. Modality metrics distinguish the task attempted, but exact-target exercises do not assess all acceptable synonyms or open-ended communication.

Export before moving devices, clearing site storage, replacing progress or deploying an update. Restore validates records and shows counts before replacement. Restoration replaces rather than merges; take a backup first. Drafts are debounced and flush on page exit. If storage is damaged, its original bytes are preserved in `wordcraft-v1-recovery`; Settings offers a recovery export. Saving is blocked until explicit valid restoration or a deliberate user reset. Do not clear recovery bytes before keeping a copy.

If another tab has a newer revision, an older tab cannot silently overwrite it. Export the older tab if needed, close it, and reload the newer state. There is no backend synchronization. Recordings remain temporary local browser objects; use Download recording to keep one. Microphone permission is requested only after pressing Record.
